The marques

Car brands, and every model under them.

Every car marque we can account for — the open VehiclesDB catalogue, plus four marques folded into it — grouped by country with every model underneath. The whole list ships with the site: opening a brand fetches nothing.

Where this comes from. Makes and models are the open VehiclesDB catalogue (CC BY 4.0), reconciled from the official registries of 14 countries, plus four marques added directly to the catalogue — Koenigsegg, Tatra, Rimac and Maruti Suzuki. Generations and engine variants come from gor3a/vehicle-makes-models (ODbL): 30,535 variants with years, body, power, torque and dimensions. Open a brand, then a model, to see them. Everything ships as files with this site — no API is called to browse.
