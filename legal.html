Privacy

CarDNA has no accounts and no backend. A VIN you decode is sent to NHTSA's public vPIC API from your browser; we never receive or store it. Recent lookups, saved vehicles and your private vault live in your browser's local storage (cardna.recent, cardna.garage, cardna.vault) and disappear when you clear site data. The first two are readable by anyone using this browser; the vault is encrypted with a pass phrase you choose and cannot be read without it. There are no analytics, advertising or tracking cookies on this site.

If a paid European decoder is added later, it will run through a proxy that keeps the API key server-side and stores only the decoded result, keyed by VIN — never your identity.

The database schema for accounts, a synced garage and an owner-only VIN list is written but not in use yet, so nothing about you reaches a server today. When it is switched on, saved vehicles will be readable only by their owner, enforced in the database rather than in the page.

Terms of use

The decoder and catalogue are provided as-is, for information. Vehicle records are filed by manufacturers and can be incomplete or wrong; a decode is not a substitute for checking the physical vehicle, its documents or a workshop diagnosis. Do not rely on a single field to order a part.

You may use the site for personal and commercial research. Do not scrape, mirror or resell the catalogue, and do not present another service's data as ours.
