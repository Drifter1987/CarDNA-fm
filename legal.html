Privacy

A VIN you decode is sent to NHTSA's public vPIC API from your browser; we do not store the vehicle record ourselves. Your account's own data lives in Supabase, in rows only you can read — enforced by the database, not by this page: your saved vehicles, your last six recent lookups, and the encrypted copy of your vault.

The vault's pass phrase never leaves your browser, and neither does the plain list: only the AES-256-GCM cipher text is stored, so the locked blob can sit in your account without anyone — us included — being able to read it. Recent lookups and the saved garage are stored readable, by you, on any device you sign in on. There are no analytics, advertising or tracking cookies on this site.

This browser also keeps a copy of each of those (cardna.garage, cardna.recent.<account>, cardna.vault.<account>) so a page renders at once and works offline; a change made offline is queued and sent when the connection is back. Clearing site data removes that copy, not your account's rows.

If a paid European decoder is added later, it will run through a proxy that keeps the API key server-side and stores only the decoded result, keyed by VIN — never your identity.

One owner account — the address named in docs/supabase-owner-act-as.sql — may open another account's view from the staff console, read-only, to help with a support request. That view cannot change anything of theirs, the database refuses every other account, and each use is recorded in the activity log.

If the account storage has not been switched on in this project yet, the page says so and keeps recent lookups and the vault in this browser only, under the keys above.

Terms of use

The decoder and catalogue are provided as-is, for information. Vehicle records are filed by manufacturers and can be incomplete or wrong; a decode is not a substitute for checking the physical vehicle, its documents or a workshop diagnosis. Do not rely on a single field to order a part.

You may use the site for personal and commercial research. Do not scrape, mirror or resell the catalogue, and do not present another service's data as ours.

A free account includes three VIN decodes and one saved vehicle. Paid plans, their European allowances and the per-lookup price beyond them are set out on the pricing page.
