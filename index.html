What a VIN actually tells you

Position 1–3 is the manufacturer and market, 4–8 the model line, engine and body, position 9 a check digit, the tenth the model year and the eleventh the plant. We read the manufacturer-filed record, not a pattern guess.

  • Exact configurationEngine, body, drive and trim as filed.
  • OriginPlant country and city where it was built.
  • Free and unlimitedNHTSA vPIC, no key and no account.

vPIC is the US federal record — non-US and pre-1981 vehicles can return “no record”.
