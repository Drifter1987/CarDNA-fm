01 / what costs money

Only one thing does

The US federal record is public domain and the catalogue is a static file, so both cost us nothing to serve. European factory data is bought per VIN at €0.22–€0.49 — that is the only number that sets a price.

02 / quota sizing

Sized for your worst month

Every quota is set so the plan still works if you use all of it: 73–74% gross at full quota, 87–91% at normal use. No plan is funded by hoping you stay under it.

03 / overage

A tap, not a wall

Past the quota you pay per lookup instead of being blocked — €0.99, €0.85 or €0.55 against a €0.26 cost. That is the same wholesale-to-retail spread the data vendor charges us.
