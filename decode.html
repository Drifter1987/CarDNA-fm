What a VIN actually tells you

A VIN is a structured record, not a random string. Position 1–3 is the manufacturer and market, 4–8 is the model line, engine and body, position 9 is a check digit, the tenth is the model year and the eleventh is the plant.

  • Exact configurationEngine, body, drive and trim as filed by the manufacturer.
  • OriginPlant country and city where the vehicle was built.
  • ShareableThe address bar carries the VIN, so a link reopens the same decode.
What we do not show. vPIC is the US federal record. Non-US vehicles, pre-1981 classics and factory option codes usually live in the OEM catalogue instead — that is the layer we license next, and the reason a VIN can decode to "no record".
